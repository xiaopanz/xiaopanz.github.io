Hello 👋! I am Xiaopan Zhang (张晓攀), a third-year Ph.D. student in Machine Learning at the <a href="https://www.gatech.edu">Georgia Institute of Technology</a>, advised by Professor <a href="https://jiachenli94.github.io">Jiachen Li</a>. My research interests include embodied AI agents, multi-robot systems, and reinforcement learning for robotics. I develop trustworthy algorithms for generalizable multi-robot coordination and robot-human collaboration in real-world environments.

My current research focuses on intervention-guided mobile manipulation: tracing failures across navigation, perception, and contact, coordinating physical recovery, and selectively adapting robot skills, residual controllers, and vision-language-action policies. I also work on foundation-model rewards for object-goal navigation and reliable communication and planning for heterogeneous robot teams.

I joined Georgia Tech in August 2026 after studying Electrical Engineering as a Ph.D. student at the <a href="https://www.ucr.edu">University of California, Riverside</a> from March 2024 to August 2026. During summer 2026, I was a Student Associate at <a href="https://usa.honda-ri.com">Honda Research Institute USA</a>, where I developed SituLaMP for multi-robot planning and recovery during execution. Previously, I was a Robotics Research Intern at AlphaZ, Inc., working on StratMamba for LiDAR-based obstacle avoidance and sim-to-real deployment.

I earned my M.S. in Computer Science from the <a href="https://www.usc.edu">University of Southern California</a> in 2023, where I conducted research on quadruped robot teams in the <a href="https://sites.usc.edu/quann/">Dynamic Robotics and Control Laboratory</a> under Professor Quan Nguyen. I received my B.S. in Computer Science from the <a href="https://www.umich.edu">University of Michigan, Ann Arbor</a> in 2021.

You can find more about my work on the [projects]({{ '/projects/' | relative_url }}), [publications]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}), and [CV]({{ '/cv/' | relative_url }}) pages. Beyond research, I contribute to open-source projects and enjoy photography and travel.
